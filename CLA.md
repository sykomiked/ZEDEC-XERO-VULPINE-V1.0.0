One agreement covers both parts of the repository: the software (Apache
License 2.0) and the hardware designs (ZXV Hardware License). It follows
the structure of the Apache Software Foundation's Individual and Corporate
CLAs, in original wording, with one addition: the right for the Project
Owner to license hardware contributions commercially, which the royalty
model needs.
