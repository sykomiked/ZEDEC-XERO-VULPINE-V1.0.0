in this file changes or adds to that licence.

**Community and noncommercial use** is free and always will be. Thank you
for building with ZXV.

**If you make money with ZXV,** we ask, as a request and not a condition,
that you contribute **18% of the gross revenue** that comes from it to:

    ILLUMAHEART FOUNDATION

    7220 N 387th Ave, Tonopah, AZ 85354, United States

Write to deal@zedec.ai to arrange a contribution or to agree a different
amount. Contributors may be acknowledged as ZXV patrons.

**Public infrastructure and the public good.** We do not ask for a
contribution when ZXV is used for critical or public infrastructure that
serves the public good, such as water, power, health, emergency services,
transport, communications or education, provided the use is not military.
Contributions from such users are welcome but never expected.

**Hardware is different.** Hardware designs under `hardware/` are under the
ZXV Hardware License, where the royalty for commercial manufacture is a
binding term. See `hardware/LICENSE`.
