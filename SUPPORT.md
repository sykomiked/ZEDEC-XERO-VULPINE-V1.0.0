# Getting support

Support for the ZXV operating system, the ready-to-run images and installers,
the bundled apps and the update and network services is provided by
**SCENIC BYPASS CONSULTING, LLC** (Arizona, United States), which licenses the
system to end users under the ZXV End-User Agreement (section 20.5).

Support tickets go to SCENIC BYPASS CONSULTING, LLC, not to 36N9 GENETICS LLC.
36N9 GENETICS LLC created ZXV and holds its copyright (see [NOTICE](NOTICE));
it does not provide support for the distributed system.

## Where to send what

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Help with the operating system, apps or services; a support ticket | SCENIC BYPASS CONSULTING, LLC, at the support contact shown on the device under Settings, About, Support |
| A bug you can reproduce | A GitHub issue in this repository, with the version and steps; SCENIC BYPASS CONSULTING, LLC triages them |
| A security problem | Never a public issue: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |
| The licence, the CLA or contributing code | [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CLA.md](CLA.md) |
| Manufacturing, partnership or a commercial hardware licence | One of the two pre-approved partners, both in Arizona: ILLUMAHEART FOUNDATION or SCENIC BYPASS CONSULTING, LLC, never 36N9 GENETICS LLC directly. 36N9 GENETICS LLC, the root organisation, partners with manufacturers under its master partnership licence |

Please include the ZXV version (Settings, About), the platform (macOS, Linux,
Windows or bare metal) and what you expected to happen.

ZXV is provided without warranty (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and the End-User
Agreement, sections 12 and 13). Support is given in good faith and does not
add a warranty.
