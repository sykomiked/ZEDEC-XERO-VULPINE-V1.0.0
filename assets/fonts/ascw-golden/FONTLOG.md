# ASCW Golden: FONTLOG

Every face here is a renamed, modified build of an upstream font under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt in this folder). The ASCW changes:
family renaming (the OFL's Reserved Font Name clause; Adobe's Source Han
fonts reserve "Source", which no ASCW name uses), harmonised vertical
metrics (most faces: ascender 0.618 em, descender 0.382 em, the golden
section), and for
the CJK faces a smaller character set. The fonts stay under the OFL; they
are not relicensed under the Apache License that covers ZXV's software.

ASCW Golden Cherokee was rebuilt for ZXV from Noto Sans Cherokee (OFL), because
the Cherokee face in the ASCW source folder was Plantagenet Cherokee, a
Tiro Typeworks font licensed with Windows, which may not be redistributed.

ASCW Golden Tibetan and ASCW Golden Thaana were made for ZXV from Noto Serif
Tibetan and Noto Sans Thaana (OFL, the google/fonts variable fonts fixed at
weight 400), renamed into the family and given its golden-section metrics,
because the ASCW source folder has no face for either script.

The members for historical and other scripts the ASCW source folder lacks
(Old Persian, Ugaritic, Linear A and B, Phoenician, Ogham, Brahmi, Tangut,
Mongolian and the rest listed below as derived from Noto) were made the same
way by tools/fonts/build_golden_from_noto.py.

ASCW Golden Mono, the fixed-width member, was made for ZXV from Noto Sans Mono
(OFL, weight 400, width 100) with the key symbols it lacks (command, option,
shift, return, delete, control, escape, caps lock) taken from Noto Sans
Symbols and Noto Sans Symbols 2 (OFL) and fitted to its advance width
(tools/fonts/build_golden_mono.py).
