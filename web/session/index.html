Your passport

Your identity is rebuilt here from your seed. Nothing is looked up and nothing leaves this page.

O reads as zero, I and L as one; a typo is caught.
At least 16 characters. Stretching it takes 64 MiB of memory and three passes.

Your passport starts as a seed: 32 random bytes made here, by this browser, and never sent anywhere. Whoever has the seed is you, on any device.

Write this down. It is shown only now, and it is the only way back to this identity. Nobody can recover it for you.
Groups, spaces and case do not matter.
