Uses: `use banker 1` (`banker.total` for a record class's bytes, `banker.entry_ok` for each record entry); `use scent 1` (`scent.score` to rank a search hit); `use keepsake 1` (`keepsake.removed`, `keepsake.keep_ok`: what a prune would take, never all); `use witness 1` (Witness's entry record and its chain-intact check over the record's history). Call these composed entries; do not re-implement them.
Logic (critical, new decisions only): retention schedule rows (class code, years, months) as fixed business records and days until a version may go (filed day + schedule days - today, refused on overflow) in COBOL as Sutra + APL as KRON witnessed; "may this version be pruned" = schedule over AND no hold AND `keepsake.keep_ok` in Ada as Eve + APL as KRON witnessed; retention limits (1 day to 36525 days, a hold always wins) in Ada as Eve; storage growth (bytes per month as a vector, projected bytes after n months from the mean monthly growth, floor) exact in FORTRAN as Trebago; records per class per year in APL as KRON (the index grid); record hierarchy (class, record, version, filed day, hold) in MUMPS as MYRA.

Tests: as the spec, plus a compose test (the app composes against a registry holding its parts, and every part entry it calls resolves) and the BOTH test above.
