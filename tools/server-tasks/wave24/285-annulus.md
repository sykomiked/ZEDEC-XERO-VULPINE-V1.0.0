Logic (critical, new decisions only): `dose_ok` (each dose and the day's total at most the prescribed maximum) in Ada as Eve + APL as KRON + FORTRAN as Trebago witnessed (three witnesses); `kinetics` (amount remaining after t hours by the declared half-life, integer halving steps) in FORTRAN as Trebago + APL as KRON witnessed; `dispensing` (dispense number, item, quantity, refused on overflow) in COBOL as Sutra; `interactions` grid (medicine by medicine) in APL as KRON; `medications` tree in MUMPS as MYRA.
BOTH test: prescriber A says 10 mg twice a day and prescriber B 20 mg once a day for the same medicine: the instruction is BOTH and paused for a pharmacist; never merged. Join with `lpres_conjoin` / `lpres_disjoin` in C; never re-derive the tables.
Clinical: advisory and human-approved; the app never changes a prescription; not a medical device.
