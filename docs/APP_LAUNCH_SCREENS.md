# App launch screens

Every ZXV app opens with the boot screen itself, played inside the window the person opened for that app. The big
top-centre logo is the app's icon instead of the OS fox, the boot's same five logos (36N9, ACOTO, the ASCW crest,
SMIC, CIS) stay on the tiles underneath, and the vault opens inside the window onto the app. Each app keeps its own
beginning, middle and end sound and a faint motion of its own behind the tiles. One screen, a theme per app, so 407
apps do not mean 407 bespoke screens.

It is an OS feature: an app maker picks a sound sequence and gives an icon, and the system does the rest. System apps'
icons are held to the palette (gold #F2C230 on #002911); an app a person makes may colour its icon however it likes.

Status: approved by the owner (11:00Z); all 407 apps in the catalogue (three tiers) have their own entry and sound sequence. Six have
hand-made sounds (Banker, Flint, Murmuration, Coffer, Cutwater, Commons); the other 138 are built from one recipe.
The owner approved the look (10:53Z) and asked for the boot's five logos on the tiles, which this version does. The logic
(`kernel/src/launch/zxl_launch.{h,c}`) is built and tested; drawing plugs into the window manager's `launch_draw` hook
(Native app toolkit, PR #110) once that lands.

## The sequence

Same grammar as the boot, so the whole system speaks one loading language:

| Step | What you see | What you hear | What drives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Begin | The app's window opens on the boot screen: its icon dim at the top, the five logos dim on their tiles, its ambient motion behind | The begin cue | The launch starting |
| Gather | Two mirrored gold bars run inward; each logo lights as the head reaches it | One pitched tick per tile, climbing the app's scale; the centre tile plays the top note | The app's **real** load, in five parts |
| Light | The icon lights and warms, one sweep of light | Nothing | All five parts loaded |
| Release | The bar under the icon grows centre-out while the vault's bolts lock across the seam | Nothing | The rest of the real load; unknown time breathes, never a fake number |
| Open | The bolts throw back and the window's face splits down the middle onto the app | The end cue | The app reporting ready, never before |

The whole sequence happens inside the app's window, not full screen: the toolkit opens the window, the launch
screen fills its content area, and the vault opens onto the app in that window. Switching between open windows
does not replay it.

A warm launch (the app is already loaded) skips straight to the vault opening and the end cue. Nothing is ever padded
to make the screen last longer than the load.

Downloads, copies and installs keep a plain green and gold bar, as before.

## What a theme carries

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Key | The app's key. The OS boot keeps E (chime, coins, gong); group defaults never use E so the boot stays the biggest moment. Murmuration, the swarm itself, is the one app in E. |
| Timbre | glass, marimba, chip, flute, bell, coin, pluck, strings |
| Icon | The app's icon in the fox's place (system apps: the owner's drawings, `zxv-brand/app-icons/NNN-name.png`, recoloured to the palette; a stand-in mark until one arrives) |
| Ambient | arches, sparks, flock, dust, water, rings: faint motion behind the tiles |

The tiles never change: they are the boot's five logos.

Every app in the catalogue has its own entry (`kernel/src/launch/zxl_apps.inc`, generated by
`tools/launch/launch_catalogue.py` from `APP_CATALOG_407.csv`). Tier 1's twelve groups are the twelve families; tier 2
and 3 apps join the family that fits what they do (health with access and safety, agriculture with places and life,
and so on). An app takes its family's timbre and motion, so a family resembles itself, and gets its own key (the 11
keys that are not the boot's E, a different one for each app in the family, then other octaves) and one of 12 phrase
shapes for its begin and end. No two apps share key, octave, timbre and phrase, and no two tier-1 apps in a group share
a scale; the test checks both. The twelve group defaults are for apps
people make that pick no sequence.

## Making an app

group default (`group1` to `group12`). Leave it out and the app
gets its group's. The app's icon goes to the top centre on its own. `zxl_theme_pick(name)` resolves the name and
`zxl_icon_ink(system_app)` says whether the icon is recoloured to the palette (system apps) or drawn as made.

## Sound setting

The owner's earlier rule was "sound only on the OS boot; app launches are silent". The 2026-10-12 ask changes
that for app launches. Launch sounds are **on by default**, with a setting to turn them off:

- Keystone > Sound > **App launch sounds** (setting key `sound.app_launch`, default on), plus a launch volume.
- Silent whenever the system is muted or a focus mode (Stillwater) is on.
- With a screen reader (Nightjar) speaking, the ticks are dropped and begin/end play ducked to 96/256, so a
  launch never talks over speech.
- Reduce motion swaps the vault for a short cross-fade.

## Files

- `kernel/src/launch/zxl_launch.{h,c}`: themes, tick notes, sound policy, and the event sequence driven by real
  progress. Integer only, freestanding. Test: `kernel/src/launch/test_zxl_launch.c` (in `make verify-all`).
- `kernel/src/zui/zui_launch_theme.{h,c}`: the live screen. Opens the app's window through the window manager
  (`zui_wm`) with the five logos and the app's icon, plays BEGIN as it opens, a tick as each tile's gold comes up
  (so the sounds move at the speed of the real load), and END when the app reports ready. The shell supplies the
  pictures and the audio output. An app built from a recipe loads by fetching its blocks: its opener calls
  zxvd `fab.receive.start`, then `fab.receive.step` until phase `"done"` (`zxvd_fab.h` B13), and hands each answer
  to `zui_launch_theme_load`. The verified blocks fill the tiles (the outer pair first, the centre last) and the
  bar never runs ahead of them; once every block is in, the mark lights and the bar above it breathes while the
  app is built (no number: the time is not known); only `"done"`, after the target CID matched, opens the doors
  and plays the end. A failed step or a cancel never does. A warm open plays only the end.
  Test: `kernel/src/zui/test_zui_launch_theme.c` (in `make verify-all`); the block mapping is unit-tested in
  `test_zxl_launch.c` (`zxl_launch_blocks`, `zxl_launch_screen_p`).
- `kernel/src/zui/zui_apphost.{h,c}`: the desktop session's app opener. Attaches the launch screens to the window
  manager, opens the app's window on its screen, then drives a `zxm_open_t` (`kernel/src/apps/zxm_open.h`) on
  the app's recipe one step a frame, each answer passed straight onto `zui_launch_theme_load`. Done tells the
  desktop where the app was written; a failure tells it why (zxvd's own words) and closes the window; closing a
  window that is still loading cancels the receive. Test: `kernel/src/zui/test_zui_apphost.c` (in
  `make verify-all`, ASan+UBSan), against a scripted zxvd.
- `kernel/src/launch/zxl_synth.{h,c}`: the sounds, made live in integers the moment each cue is called for, from
  the app's theme: the same notes, timbres, pans, echoes and fades as `launch_sounds.py` (the six hand-made apps
  keep their own recipes). Every other app's phrase carries a quiet layer in its tier's material: dry wood
  and relay clicks for the 144 base apps, a latch, an escapement's tick-tock and a furnace hum for the
  composites, glass and bells for the crystal apps. Cues overlap freely and are pulled by the audio device as it has room; an app's end
  is prepared in the background while it loads, so it sounds the moment the app is ready. 48 kHz stereo S16.
  Test: `kernel/src/launch/test_zxl_synth.c` (every app's tick in its own note, all 407 begins different,
  levels, pans, ducking, and cues starting exactly when called).
- `kernel/src/zui/zui_image.{h,c}`: reads PNG files (every colour type and depth, any zlib stream, tRNS),
  keys out the ZXV green the icons are drawn on, crops to the drawn part and fits it to a box, streaming row by
  row (a 3800 px logo needs a few rows of memory). Draws at any strength, in its own colours or held to gold.
  Test: `kernel/src/zui/test_zui_image.c` (pixel-exact fixtures from `tools/zui/make_png_fixtures.py`, damaged
  files under ASan and UBSan).
- `kernel/src/zui/zui_launch_shell.{h,c}`: the desktop's side: give it a file reader, the icons and logos
  folders and the audio device, attach it with `zui_launch_theme_attach(..., zui_ls_shell(ls), ...)` and call
  `zui_ls_pump` once a frame. Icons are read from disk the first time an app opens (`zxl_icon_file`:
  `001-burrow.png`, `l2/145-dragon-archivist.png`, ...; an app a person made: `<name>.png`), so a redrawn icon
  shows on the next launch and no picture is built into the system. Vectors first: given a vector drawer
  (`zui_vec_draw_simple` from the toolkit's ZXVG renderer, through a three-line adapter), every icon and logo is
  looked for first as `.zxvg` beside its PNG (`001-burrow.zxvg`, `logos/ascw-gold.zxvg`, built with
  `tools/zxvg/svg2zxvg.py` from `zxv-brand/app-icons/svg/` and `zxv-brand/vector/gold/`) and drawn live at the
  window's own size, exactly as drawn (each file carries its plate and palette); the PNG is only the fallback
  for a missing or malformed vector. Each vector is drawn once per size (on black and on white, the difference
  its coverage) and blended every frame after, so a launch frame is a blit. The tiles are small, so the crest
  installed as `logos/ascw-gold.zxvg` is the lighter `vector/gold/ascw-small.svg` (46 KB; the full seal is for
  the big boot frames). Test: `kernel/src/zui/test_zui_launch_shell.c`
  (a whole launch: files read, gold icon for a system app, own colours for a person's app, five ticks as five
  tiles light, the end the moment the app is ready, sound reaching the audio device, mute, missing files,
  vectors before PNGs and the fallback when a vector is malformed).
- `tools/launch/launch_sounds.py`: synthesises each app's three cues from scratch (no samples), deterministic.
- `tools/launch/launch_mock.py`: renders the mockup videos with their sounds placed where the events happen.
- `tools/launch/launch_catalogue.py`: `table` regenerates `zxl_apps.inc` from the app list; `render --new` renders every
  app's launch video and re-renders only those whose icon arrived since, so it can run after each icon import.

```sh
python3 tools/launch/launch_sounds.py out/sounds          # begin / middle / end WAVs per app
python3 tools/launch/launch_mock.py out [burrow flint ...] # MP4 with sound + stills (needs ffmpeg)
python3 tools/launch/launch_catalogue.py render APP_CATALOG_407.csv out --new   # every app; only new icons on re-runs
# ZXV_APP_ICONS=<dir of NNN-name.png>  ZXV_DESKTOP=<desktop png behind the window>  ZXV_FONT=<ttf>
```
