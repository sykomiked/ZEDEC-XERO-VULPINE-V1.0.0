## 1. Design Philosophy — the axioms the whole system obeys

ZXV is built from a small set of non-negotiable design axioms. Every module in the tree is written to them, and the build's grep-gates and host tests enforce several of them mechanically.

1. **One number type, exact on the target.** All value, surplus, price, weight, and probability arithmetic uses `surplus_real_t`: an IEEE `double` under the host test build (`-DTEST_HOST`) and a **Q32.32 fixed-point `int64`** on the freestanding target. Code never touches the representation directly — it goes through the `SR_` macros (`SR_ADD`, `SR_MUL`, `SR_DIV`, `SR_CMP`, …). The **fraction-first rule** — `SR_MUL(x, SR_DIV(n, d))`, never `SR_DIV(SR_MUL(x, n), d)` — keeps intermediate products inside `int64` on the target, an overflow the host `double` build cannot see. There is no floating-point unit dependency and no `malloc` on the target.

2. **Phase-tick ordering, never the wall clock.** Causality in the system is expressed as monotonic **phase ticks** advanced explicitly (`con_tick`, the phase coordinator, subterminal sync). Nothing branches on real time; determinism is a property, not an aspiration. The same inputs produce the same verdicts across a thousand repeated evaluations (an actual test).

3. **No hollow capabilities — ops boundaries fail closed.** Wherever the system must consult a fact it cannot compute for itself — a delivered shipment, an external attestation, a market outcome, a random seed — that fact enters through a **bindable hook** (a function pointer on the state). When the hook is **unbound, the operation fails closed**: escrow stays shut, distribution is refused, money does not move. The kernel verifies the *signature or attestation over* the fact (Ed25519, keyed digests, content addresses); it never fabricates the real-world fact, and it never trusts a bare caller-set boolean. Every host test asserts against an **external anchor** — an RFC/FIPS vector, a known answer, or a conservation identity — not against the code's own output.

4. **Tri-Space typing.** Every artifact carries a valence in a three-space triad: **S+ (`.zxvc`, positive)**, **S− (`.cedez`, negative)**, **S0 (`.cedec`, neutral)**. The triad is a first-class type, not a tag, and it flows through fusion, files, and judgment (Wyrmgate).

5. **Sovereignty by construction.** Every system is a **sovereign node** — a microstate — and every user a sovereign individual. There is no central kill switch anywhere in the tree (the One Policy voids terms that carry one), no platform ban primitive (it was never written, and the build greps to prove it), and no advertising primitive (likewise). Safety is achieved by *matching like with like*, not by silencing.

6. **The One Policy is definitional, not administrative.** A term that produces asymmetric harm, unilateral extraction, usury, or a non-reciprocal burden **is, by definition, not a term** — the Symbiotic Maxim excludes it locally and deterministically, with no clock, network, or central authority. This predicate (`op_evaluate`) is composed by the economy, governance, and logistics layers rather than re-implemented.

## 3. Axiomatic Core & the Q32.32 Math Substrate

This domain is the arithmetic bedrock and the causal spine every other subsystem stands on. It answers two questions freestandingly — with no libc, no `malloc`, and no hardware float on the AArch64 target: *what number type does the kernel reason in*, and *in what order are coupled steps allowed to happen*.

### The real-number substrate

`surplus.h` defines `surplus_real_t` and its `SR_` macro family. This is the house invariant that must never be hand-rolled around: on `TEST_HOST` the type is `double` and `SR_MUL`/`SR_LN` map to libm; on target it is a `Q32.32` fixed-point `int64_t` where `SR_ONE == (1<<32)` and every operation routes through `sr_mul_impl`/`sr_div_impl` (128-bit intermediates on any 64-bit target, hand-split long division as the 32-bit fallback). Two red-team-hardened decisions define it: `SR_FROM_INT` *multiplies* by `SR_ONE` rather than left-shifting, because shifting a negative `int64` is UB that `sr_ln()` hits for every argument below 1; and `sr_div_impl` scales through a 128-bit numerator, fixing a prior overflow that silently returned 0 and violated ISF normalization axiom S4 (`f(1,N)=ln N`). Transcendentals are built from primitives — `sr_ln` via range reduction plus a 21-term `t=(m-1)/(m+1)` series, `sr_sqrt` via 20 Newton iterations.

Exactness where it is achievable lives in three rational types, each fit to purpose: `rat_t` (WyvernEye) is exact ℚ arithmetic (`rat_make`, `rat_add`, `rat_div`) that *detects* overflow into a `valid=false` flag rather than wrapping; `rmag_rational_t` carries always-reduced resource budgets (`rmag_rational_reduce`); and `rational_t` in `m5_types.h` is the manifold's magnitude axis.

### The axiomatic functionals

`surplus.c` implements the Interaction Surplus Framework: `surplus_u` computes `u = 1-(x·y)²` and `surplus_f` evaluates `f(u)=ln(1+(N-1)u)`, guarding `N<2` and Q32.32 overflow before touching `SR_LN`. On top sit decomposition, Lipschitz stability, and the open-system dynamics `surplus_dynamics_step` (`Q_{t+1}=(1-δ)Q_t+ηS_t-C_t`). `edp_risk` lifts this into the **M⁵ Curzi Manifold** `m5_coords_t = ℕ×ℝ⁺×[0,1]×iℝ×Χ`, with the risk operator `edp_compute_risk : M⁵ → ℂ`, the coverage hyperbola `edp_coverage_satisfied` (`r·ℓ ≥ 1.8`, stored as `18/10`), zero-preserving division `edp_zpd`, and `edp_fibonacci`-signed paradox algebra. The identical five axes reappear as `phase_tick_t {omega, r, ell, iphi, chi}` and as the composite key of `axiom_matrix` — `axiom_matrix_project_tick` reads the manifold as a sparse complex field indexed by the full tuple.

### The ordering spine

Causality, not wall-clock, orders everything: `oseq` (K1) assigns monotonic ordinals over a causal DAG with `oseq_happens_before` and replay rejection; `phase_coord` (K6) is the admission gate whose `pc_admit` returns `ADMIT/VETO/DEFER/RETRY` over `current_logical_time`. This is the OPS-BOUNDARY fail-closed discipline in code: unknown or degraded health *defers*, and a `is_hardware_action` step demands both `pc_check_all_gates` full coverage and `PC_HEALTH_HEALTHY` or it is vetoed.

### How it composes

The Tri-Space triad is native here: `lpres` (K3) four-valued paraconsistent presence (`NEITHER/TRUE/FALSE/BOTH` → S0/S+/S−/glut, via `lpres_conjoin` and `lpres_safety_gate_clear`) maps directly onto `trit_t`'s GLUT states and `trit_to_ell`. `rmag` (K2) meters budgets, `iphase` (K4) routes, and `choice` (K5) resolves ties — all deferring ambiguity to S0. The speculative `superpos`, `dualtrack`, and `lattice` build/execution layers borrow the same GLUT vocabulary and phase-tick clock (`cycle_pulse` explicitly replacing the wall clock), collapsing eigenstates only at tick boundaries. Every downstream domain — governance, refinery, apps — ultimately computes in `surplus_real_t` and is sequenced by K6.

## 4. Architecture, Boot, Memory & Scheduling

This domain is the AArch64 substrate everything else stands on: the reset-vector-to-`[BOOT_OK]` chain, the EL1/EL0 privilege split, the page tables, the interrupt/timer path, the process scheduler, and the persistent store. The primary (and, on this host, only booting) target is `qemu-system-aarch64 -M virt`; the x86 `mm.h`/`syscall.h`/`timer.h` (32-bit `page_directory_t`, `int 0x80` dispatch, PIT) are legacy and not on the ARM64 boot path.

**Boot chain.** `boot.s` `_start` runs at EL2, seeds `SCTLR_EL1`, sets `HCR_EL2.RW`, grants EL1 the physical timer via `CNTHCTL_EL2`, and `eret`s to `el1_entry`. There it points `VBAR_EL1` at `vector_table`, enables FP/SIMD in `CPACR_EL1` (`-O2` auto-vectorizes even page-table zeroing, which would otherwise trap), sets `sp`, clears BSS, and `bl kernel_main` → `kernel_main_arm64()`. That function is the deterministic bring-up sequence: `uart_init`, banner, `arm64_mmu_init`, `gic_init` + `gic_register_handler(IRQ_TIMER, arm64_timer_handler)`, `arm64_timer_init`, then the M5 core (`phase_coordinator_init(&tick, EXEC_DC)`, `rmag_init`, `lpres_init`, `iphase_init`, `oseq_init`), the economic/app subsystems, storage, `sched_init`, optional EL0, and finally `[BOOT_OK] Phase E0082 complete`, `boot_features_init`, `boot_economy_init`.

**MMU.** `arm64_mmu.c` builds static `l0_table`/`l1_table`/`l2_table` (no `malloc`): a 1 GB L2 identity map at 2 MB granularity plus 1 GB L1 blocks anchored at `board_get_profile()->ram_base`. Device-vs-Normal attribution is *computed*, not hardcoded — `block_overlaps_periph()` pads each board's real `uart_base`/`gicd_base`/`gicr_base`/`virtio_mmio_base` by 16 MB and errs toward Device, so a MediaTek Tank profile can't silently leave MMIO cacheable. `TCR_EL1` uses `T0SZ=24` (walk starts at L0); `MAIR_EL1` indexes Normal/Device. `arm64_copy_pt_template()` clones the kernel map into per-process tables.

**Interrupts & tick.** `gicv3.c` `gic_init()` enables the distributor with `ARE | ENABLE_G1`, routes SPIs *and* the timer PPI (`IRQ_TIMER=30`) into Group 1 through the redistributor SGI frame after the `GICR_WAKER` handshake. `gic_handle_irq()` acks via `ICC_IAR1_EL1` and EOIs via `ICC_EOIR1_EL1`. `arm64_timer.c` programs the generic timer at 100 Hz; `arm64_timer_handler()` reloads `CNTP_TVAL` and fires the registered `timer_cb`.

**EL1/EL0 & scheduling.** `el0_userspace.h` defines the real isolation boundary: each `user_proc_t` owns an L0 table in `TTBR0_EL1` while the kernel stays in shared, never-switched `TTBR1_EL1`. `proc_create`/`proc_create_from_elf` build address spaces; the `irq_handler_lower_aarch64` vector calls `el0_irq_handler_c` → `proc_sched_tick(&ps, ctx)` for round-robin preemption; `SVC` lands in `proc_handle_svc`, gated by per-process `CAP_*` bits (`CAP_APP_DEFAULT` vs `CAP_TRUSTED_ALL`) with `copy_from_user`/`proc_user_range_ok` validating every user pointer (audit finding P0-1). EL0 is gated behind `ENABLE_EL0_USERSPACE`; when off, the classic `scheduler_t` (`sched_init`, `sched_create_task`) is a task registry mirroring long-lived kernel tasks for `ps`/`vmstat`, and `kernel_event_cycle_run()` — advanced by the phase tick, never wall-clock — is the heartbeat that keeps `[BOOT_OK]` deterministic.

**Storage & updates.** `zxvfs.h` is a redo-journaled flat FS on a `block_device_t` (virtio-blk in production): stage sectors → write commit header → checkpoint, replayed idempotently on `zxvfs_mount` (`journal_replays`), so a crash leaves the volume fully before or fully after a transaction. `abupdate.h` layers A/B slots (`ab_state_t`, `ab_stage_update`/`ab_slot_to_run`/`ab_confirm`/`ab_rollback`/`ab_boot_tick`) with probation and auto-rollback, all state journaled through ZXVFS. Execution is fail-closed: `zsp_verify()` checks SHA-256 integrity + Ed25519 signature against a compiled-in root public key before any `.zsp` runs; absent a virtio-blk device, persistence simply `[SKIP]`s to the volatile RAM disk.

**Composition & invariants.** Ordering is phase-tick, not time — `event_clock.h` is a Lamport-style logical clock (`event_ordinal` ≥ `local_counter`) that projects wall time only on an accepted `event_clock_sync`, its `m5.phi` saturating because `surplus_real_t` is Q32.32 on target. No libc/float on target: page tables and FS buffers are static. The layer feeds the M5 core its tick, hands EL0 processes to the ELF/`.zsp` loader and ZXVFS, enforces the OPS-boundary/fail-closed discipline (unsigned code never executes, unbound devices skip), and finally hands `boot_economy_init` the sovereign-node/One-Policy layer once the machine is live.

Grounding files: `kernel/arch/arm64/{boot.s, arm64_mmu.c, gicv3.c, arm64_timer.c, kernel_main_arm64.c, el0_userspace.h}` and `kernel/src/{zxvfs/zxvfs.h, loader/abupdate.h, loader/zsp.h, clock/event_clock.h, sched/sched.h, mm/mm.h, bootfeat/boot_features.h}`.

## 5. Security, Crypto & Admission Control

This domain is the kernel's trust boundary: the freestanding cryptographic primitives, the post-quantum key exchange, the vault that holds secrets at rest, and the two admission gates — one for peers, one for daemons — plus the role-and-authorization framework that governs offensive tooling. Everything here honors the house rule that on target there is no libc, no `malloc`, and no float; primitives are single-file and self-contained.

**Cryptographic floor.** `robin_debanks/` carries the vetted symmetric/asymmetric floor: `sha256` (streaming `sha256_init`/`update`/`final`, added specifically because two prior HMACs silently truncated past 256 bytes), `aes256_gcm_encrypt`/`decrypt` (NIST SP 800-38D, validated against GCM test case 13), and `ed25519_verify`. `mlkem/` is the post-quantum layer: `mlkem768_keygen`/`encaps`/`decaps` (FIPS 203, Fujisaki–Okamoto with implicit rejection, so a decapsulation failure is indistinguishable from success — no decryption oracle). The design split is explicit in the headers: AES-256/SHA-256 stay quantum-resistant under Grover, so only public-key operations migrate to ML-KEM/ML-DSA.

**The malleability guard.** A load-bearing invariant lives in `ed25519_s_is_canonical`: before any verify, the scalar `S = signature[32..63]` is rejected unless `S < L` (the group order, little-endian), walked most-significant-byte-first in constant time. Without it, `(R, S+L)` is a second valid encoding of the same signature — a red-team-found malleability. Only after this check does `orlp_ed25519_verify` run. The embedded roots of trust (`ED25519_PUBKEY_IMMIGRATION`, `_PORTER_HOUSE`, `_COUNT_HOUSE`, `_COMMUNITY_CHEST`, `_AI_LAYER`) are PUBLIC keys; the private halves are never compiled in.

**The vault.** `robin_vault_t` stores up to `ROBIN_MAX_ENTRIES` `robin_entry_t` records, each AES-256-GCM-sealed with a monotonic `nonce_counter` (guaranteeing unique GCM nonces), a 168-bit truncated-SHA-256 `integrity_hash`, and a GCM tag verified on every `robin_unlock`. Entries can be time-locked (`robin_request_unlock` starts a delay measured in cycles, never wall-clock), and failed unlocks past `ROBIN_MAX_ATTEMPTS` transition to `ROBIN_ENTRY_LOCKED_OUT`. The vault holds a `porter_house_t *porter` so access requests are themselves gated.

**Admission — peers and daemons.** `porter_house/` is a per-port doorman: `porter_house_admit` decides against a `ph_seal_mode_t` (`OPEN`/`TRUSTED`/`ALLOWLIST`/`CLOSED`) using a caller-supplied trust weight — deliberately decoupled from Count House. `immigration/` gates executable daemons: `immig_apply_visa` grants `RESIDENT`/`WORKER`/`TRANSIT` visas only after `immig_verify_sig` checks an Ed25519 signature over content-hash‖pubkey‖id against the embedded immigration key. This is the OPS-BOUNDARY discipline: the `verify_sig` hook FAILS CLOSED — an unbound or invalid signature yields `IMMIG_DAEMON_REJECTED` (undocumented), and repeat violations trigger `immig_deport`.

**Hat-color model.** `mage/` reifies the principle that a port scanner on your own network is diagnostics and on a non-consenting target is an attack — the tool is identical, the ENGAGEMENT differs. Hats (`MAGE_HAT_WHITE`…`GREY`) grant capability sets; OPEN capabilities (harden, detect, audit) run freely, while GATED ones (`SCAN`, `EXPLOIT`, `C2`, `LATERAL`, `EXFIL`) require an active `mage_engagement_t` whose scope covers the target. `mage_authorize` is the single gate every capability must call; with no covering engagement it returns `MAGE_NEEDS_ENGAGEMENT`, never a silent allow. Engagements are `mage_engage_self` (self-authorized over `owned` assets) or `mage_engage_signed` (external signature); windows are `[from_tick, until_tick]` phase ordinals. `mage_emulate` runs a technique and reports CAUGHT/GAP — a finding, not a foothold. Every decision appends to a hash-chained audit log (`mage_audit_verify`), and containment (`mage_contain`, from `src/concord`) is rehabilitative: a contained hat keeps defensive capabilities.

**License.** `license/` embeds the Apache License 2.0 identity and attribution notice into the binary; `license_travels_together` is false (derivatives may be relicensed under Apache-2.0's terms) and `license_precedence_rank` is 0 for the single licence.

**Composition.** `porter_house_t` pointers thread through both vault and immigration; `tls/` builds a TLS 1.3 client atop `x25519` + `hkdf` + `aead`, with `TLS_VERIFY_REQUIRED` failing closed on unverified certs, and is the natural home for the hybrid `x25519`+`mlkem768` exchange. `panopticon/` rates watchers for transparency; `smap`/`recon`/`ubh` supply content-addressed identity that these gates key on.

## 6. Economy & Finance Engine

The Economy & Finance Engine is ZXV's native, no-debt money layer: value is *equity, never debt*, so no path in the module charges interest or forces a liquidation. Everything is freestanding — integer-only `surplus_real_t` (Q32.32 on target, `double` on host) computed fraction-first through the `SR_` macros, fixed-size arrays, no libc/malloc/float, and phase-tick ordering rather than wall-clock (`fm_quote_t.phase_tick`).

**Core data model.** Two capital taxonomies coexist by design: `zcapital.h`'s `zcap_form_t` is the *canonical* nine forms (four inalienable Crown forms — Social/Natural/Cultural/Spiritual — plus five priceable ones), while `vino.h`'s older `capital_type_t` (`CAP_MAX == 9`) still backs `triple_ledger` and interop. The ledger core is the triple-rail `triple_ledger_t`: `LEDGER_FINANCIAL` (rational/debit-credit), `LEDGER_PROVENANCE` (logical/attestation), and `LEDGER_EXTERNALITY` (imaginary/relationship phase), each `ledger_entry_t` carrying its full M⁵ coordinates. On top of it, `vino_stores_t` is the settlement engine — it never re-implements double-entry, it posts *through* `triple_ledger_post` as three atomic rails under real ISO 4217 numeric codes: `VINO_ISO_DEBIT` 846 (asset/backing), `VINO_ISO_CREDIT` 888 (claim), and `VINO_ISO_EQUITY` 999 (the live equity witness, deliberately ISO XXX "no currency"). Money circulates as `floating_voucher_t`/`vino_voucher_t` — merit-based, no-debt bearer instruments on a Fibonacci denomination ladder (`vino_ladder_denomination`).

**Load-bearing invariants.** (1) *Single-active-state*: a Vino unit is spendable on exactly one rail — physical bearer note or digital ledger-ghost — enforced by `vino_can_spend`/`vino_single_active_state_ok`, so supply can never double. (2) *System-solvency gate*: `vino_ledger_act` is the choke-point; after the three rails post, the whole ledger must still satisfy coverage = assets/liabilities ≥ 1.8× **and** equity ≥ 0, or the event does a full rollback restoring every mutated scalar (`VINO_ERR_COVERAGE`/`VINO_ERR_EQUITY`) — no partial write. (3) *Usury veto*: a claimed equity above backing implies interest and is refused via `op_symbiotic_ok` → `VINO_ERR_USURY`, the One Policy / Symbiotic Maxim in force. (4) *Double-redeem guard*: a redeemed/retired voucher is terminal (`VINO_ERR_STATE`). (5) *Inalienability wall*: `zcap_exchange` conserves value and returns `ZCAP_INALIENABLE` for any Crown form — you can neither buy nor sell a language.

**The Battering Ram** (`br_exchange_t`) is a posted-rate, principal-only venue — no order book, no margin, no margin-call path. `br_swap_settle` is atomic (both legs at the *supplied* `agreed_rate` or neither); a Financial leg with no bound rail returns `BR_ERR_NO_RAIL`, closing the `FINANCIAL→X→FINANCIAL` mint attack. Outcomes are an OPS BOUNDARY: `br_distribute` fails closed (`BR_ERR_NO_ORACLE`) when no `verify` hook is bound and only verifies the *attestor's signature*, never the fact; non-achievement is `BR_FORGIVEN` with zero debt. `symbiotic_ok` voids asymmetric harm, non-reciprocity, and derivatives on suffering.

**Substrate & interop.** `alloc` provides conserved symbiotic distribution plus a kill-switch-free platform token (`ptoken_ledger_t`, append-only, double-spend refused via `PTOKEN_ERR_SPENT`, no seizure function by construction). `abacus`/`smaug_clear` does exact multilateral mutual-credit netting (conservation and net-position preservation asserted). `count_house_t` computes anti-Sybil trust-weighted floor valuations feeding `mesh_token` settlement (Porter House-gated); `financial`/`finance_markets` price instruments and reuse those pricers (`br_price_capital_future` composes `financial_price_future`, never a new pricer). `iso20022` serializes PACS.008/CAMT.053 (never sends), mirroring the 846/888/999 codes; `community_chest` links a `vino_ledger_t` for voucher cash-in/out. Every external fact enters through a bindable, fail-closed hook — the kernel verifies signatures and CIDs, never the real-world value they assert.

## 7. Governance, the One Policy & Sovereign Nodes

This is the constitutional layer: not a wall of prose but a **decidable predicate** plus a set of deliberately-separated organs that compose it. Every exchange, credential action, and settlement in ZXV passes through it.

**The One Policy — the Symbiotic Maxim as a pure predicate.** `op_evaluate(const op_term_t *t)` reduces "any term producing asymmetric harm, unilateral extraction, or non-reciprocal burden is, by definitional exclusion, not a term" to one function over `op_term_t` (`give_a/give_b`, `harm_a/harm_b`, `interest`, and boolean flags). It returns an `op_verdict_t` by checking gates in a fixed, deterministic order: `fraud_root` (Return Doctrine) → `interest > 0` (no usury) → `denies_aid | revoke_for_nonpayment | has_kill_switch` (Grace of Somalia / no coercion) → asymmetric harm → unilateral extraction → non-reciprocal burden. It is freestanding, stateless, allocation-free, clock-free, integer-only (`surplus_real_t` via `SR_` macros, `net_of` computed fraction-safe). The load-bearing invariant: the extraction gate `if (a_gives != b_gives)` fires **regardless of the caller-asserted `reciprocal` flag** — the modeled values are ground truth; a `reciprocal=true` that conveys nothing back is an attacker's lie the predicate ignores. `op_symbiotic_ok`, `op_grace_of_somalia_ok`, and `op_return_doctrine_reverts` are the composable entry points other modules call.

**Anti-capture: two organs, one membrane.** The Crown Pillar (`crown_t`/`crown_isc_t`) issues Individual Sovereign Credentials and verifies signatures — `crown_register`, `crown_isc_verify`, and the access gate `crown_isc_is_active`. It holds **no private key** (issued `sig` is zeroed; offline HSM signs), never `#include`s any ministry/ledger header, and references no money-moving symbol (verified by `make verify-all`). `crown_revoke` routes every proffered ground through `op_grace_of_somalia_ok`, returning `CROWN_ERR_COERCION` for `NONPAYMENT`/`KILL_SWITCH`/`DENY_AID`; only a fraud root or a Maxim verdict is a valid ground. The Ministry Pillar (`ministry_t`) is the half that touches money — it *wraps* `triple_ledger_t` rather than reinventing accounting, and `ministry_settle` passes every settlement through the One Policy (`interest > 0` → `MIN_VOID_MAXIM`) and fails closed on coverage `< 1.8x` → `MIN_UNDERCOVERED`, leaving the ledger untouched. It holds no signing key: a treasury cannot revoke what it cannot issue. Exchange ratios are an **OPS boundary** — `ministry_set_exchange_ratio` supplies them; they are never discovered on-device.

**Every node a microstate.** `sovereign_node_t` wraps the real `microstate_t`; `sovereign_constitute` derives `identity_cid = ipfs(LE(self) || charter_cid)`, real *before* any peer recognizes it. There is deliberately **no function that un-makes a node** — `sovereign_is_sovereign` has no false path. `sovereign_federate` composes `microstate_recognize`, which recomputes the peer's charter (`ZXV_ECHARTER`) and declaratory writ binding (`ZXV_EBADSIG`) — recognition theatre is prohibited — and touches only the recognizer's set: *between*, never *over* (`peer` is `const`).

**Interspace — the space between spaces, under Lex Rhodia.** The interstitial medium is *res communis*: `interstitial_region_t.owner` is pinned to `ZXV_NODE_NONE` and `interstitial_claim` has no success path. `lex_rhodia_general_average` apportions sacrifice by stake with exact conservation — the numeric antidote to the Maxim's asymmetric harm; sovereign immunity is "your hash is your key" (`vessel_flag_verify`); `cojurisdiction_eval` (flag-state co-jurisdiction) allows an action only when the host term is `op_symbiotic_ok` **and** the vessel's content still matches its flag. The `minister_t` *computes* fair contributions for all parties but binds only those who consented by joining the constellation; `minister_may_seize` always returns false.

**How it composes.** Wyrmgate's `wyrm_judge` renders the Tri-Space verdict (S+/`.zxvc` commit, S−/`.cedez` reject, S0/`.cedec` defer) over rmag/lpres/chiglet. `denconnect` applies node-local sovereignty — operator-defined privilege bitmasks keyed by Ed25519 identity (`den_can`, `den_may_connect`), with escalation structurally barred (only a `SET_ACCESS` holder changes rules). `legal_engine` generates the declaratory sovereign declarations and treaties these organs cite — authority claimed by content-binding, never by fiat.

## 8. Social Fabric & Reputation

This domain governs how sovereign nodes meet, how boundaries are enforced without coercion, how reputation is surfaced without silencing, and how every judgment is remembered. Four modules stack: `concord` (the matching commons), `social_spaces` (named public rooms), `reputation` (the Pig Badge and earnable badges), and `chronicle` (the tamper-evident ledger of verdicts).

**The commons and the ISF core.** `con_commons_t` holds a fixed array of `con_person_t` (interest vector, `standing_score`, `sociability` budget) plus a table of `con_divide_t`. The load-bearing decision is that introductions are ranked by *interaction surplus*, not engagement: `con_surplus` calls `chg_interaction` to compute `u = 1 - (x·y)²`, maximal for complementary (orthogonal) interests and zero at *both* extremes — echo chamber and clash alike. `con_recommend` gathers eligible peers and selection-sorts by that surplus descending; there is deliberately no recency, retention, or time-on-platform term anywhere. Surplus arithmetic is `surplus_real_t` (Q32.32 on target, `double` on host via `SR_` macros), integer-only, no float.

**Non-coercion as an invariant, not a policy.** Three symmetries make abuse of the layer unexpressible. (1) A crossed boundary produces a *mutual, temporary* `con_divide_t`; `con_divided` and `con_can_see` are strictly symmetric, so a one-way block that keeps watching cannot be written. (2) `con_may_match` gates on standing pools: the XOR test `(sa == CON_QUARANTINED) != (sb == CON_QUARANTINED)` means a quarantined person matches *only* other quarantined people — a bully meets bullies — and `con_tick` recovers `standing_score` each event cycle, so quarantine is rehabilitative, not a scarlet letter. (3) The distinct-reporter consensus rule lives inside `con_report_boundary`: the standing penalty (`CON_BOUNDARY_HIT`) is applied only *inside* the guard that creates a new divide, so a repeat report by the same reporter is a no-op. One person can never unilaterally quarantine another — it takes real, distinct consensus.

**Free rooms over the commons.** `soc_world_t` embeds one `con_commons_t` as its single safety mechanism and adds spaces and membership. The mandate is true by construction: there is no `soc_ban`, `soc_delete`, or `soc_silence` — they were never written, so tests can grep-prove it. `soc_post` deliberately does not check standing (a quarantined heckler still posts; posts are monotonic, never erased). `soc_report` is first-party only, forwarding to `con_report_boundary`. The one moderation lever, `soc_sort`, *fails closed*: it returns `SOC_ERR_NOT_QUARANTINED` unless the target was *already* quarantined through genuine reports — it imposes no quarantine and never reports on anyone's behalf, refusing to manufacture consensus. All it then does is `soc_regroup_quarantine` to seat fellow-quarantined people in one corner. No shadowban exists.

**Reputation surfaces.** `rep_state_t` carries two separate ledgers. The Pig Badge is self-blind by design: the raw `pig_level` field must be read only through `pig_level_seen_by`, which returns 0 when `viewer == subject` — the operative wears a badge the whole room reads and they cannot. `pig_flag` sits on an OPS BOUNDARY: it *records* a confirmed-abuse occurrence supplied by the upstream counter-intel pipeline (fail-closed when the table is full), never deciding guilt itself. Earnable badges (`badge_award`, `badge_score`) are monotonic and kept strictly apart — shame and score are separate ledgers.

**Memory of judgment.** `chronicle` composes Wyrmgate: `chronicle_submit` calls `wyrm_judge`, appends S+/S− verdicts to a SHA-256 hash chain (`head_k = SHA256(prev ‖ seq ‖ verdict ‖ reason ‖ digest)`), and parks S0 DEFER events in a pending set. `chronicle_poke` re-judges pending events when their blocker clears, so a deferred decision resolves itself — self-resolving, phase-tick ordered by event ordinal, never wall-clock. `chronicle_verify` recomputes the chain to catch tampering, closing the audit loop the rest of the fabric relies on.

## 9. AI, Chiglet & the Creative Suite

This domain is ZXV's answer to "native, on-device intelligence and authorship without an LLM, without the cloud, and without a single line of floating point on target." Its spine is the **Chiglet** (`src/chiglet`), a bounded mixture of tiny linear experts whose combination weights come from the Interaction Surplus Framework rather than softmax. The organizing claim: a companion should decide by how much genuinely *independent* evidence it holds, not by how loudly correlated predictors agree.

The core abstraction is `chg_result_t`, computed by `chg_infer` over `k` evidence vectors of dimension `CHG_DIM`. The load-bearing quantity is `R`, the participation ratio of the evidence Gram spectrum (`R = K'^2 / SUM(e_i.e_j)^2`): 1 under total collinearity, `K'` under orthogonality. `chg_interaction` yields the scale-invariant `u = 1 - (a.b)^2/(|a|^2|b|^2)` — computed from unnormalized vectors, with *no square root anywhere in the gate* (an inverse-sqrt carried ~1e-4 error, four orders worse than the decision margin). Two abstention rules gate a `CHG_DECIDED` verdict: `R < R_min` returns `CHG_REASON_REDUNDANT`; a small top-two `margin` returns `CHG_REASON_LOW_MARGIN`. Otherwise the runtime returns a first-class `CHG_UNCERTAIN`. The security-critical subtlety: `R` is *not* monotone under duplication, so `chg_effective_experts` first collapses directions closer than `CHG_U_MERGE` into groups, gating on *distinct* directions that cloning cannot inflate. All arithmetic uses `surplus_real_t` through `SR_` macros (Q32.32 on target, double on host); the model is a caller-authenticated `chg_model_t` with an `epoch` rollback counter and a `caps` capability mask — inference does nothing it was not granted.

**Cards** (`src/cards`) turn this math into a product. `card_evidence` derives one deterministic evidence direction per card from its discipline/gematria/root, so `loadout_equip` + `loadout_apply` + `loadout_infer` make "collect broadly, not deeply" a *geometric* fact: eight duplicate cards still leave `R = 1`. The card's printed shape is the dataflow circuit — `sigil_t` carries a star polygon `{N/k}` (the circulant graph `C(N,{k})`) whose `sig_fabric_lanes` equals `gcd(N,k)`: the arithmetic of the drawn figure *is* its parallelism, scheduled by dependency waves in `sig_schedule` (phase-order, never clock). `zca` is the activation VM: `zca_derive`/`zca_assemble` converge byte-identically, and `zca_exec` writes only into a caller-supplied `card_effect_t` — total (no jumps/loops), address-free, bounded, and CRC16-`SEAL`ed so an OCR misread fails loudly rather than executing a different valid program.

The **Refinery** (`src/refinery`, `enochian`, `gematria`) is the forge: `ref_forge` compiles intent text in any language via `eno_gematria`->`eno_root`->star fabric, and `sha256`->kamea circuit, into a `ref_card_t`. `ref_preset_pack`/`ref_preset_unpack` serialize a card to a small sealed blob that any peer re-derives and re-verifies from the text alone, refusing tampered claims with `REF_ERR_FORGERY` — a shareable AI preset with no server holding anything. Its three voices `ENO_VOICE_SOLAR`/`LUNAR`/`AEON` map exactly onto the Tri-Space triad S+/S-/S0.

The creative suite wraps the same core in honest OPS boundaries. `art_studio` is a real 256x256 integer raster core (`art_compose_sigil`, `art_export_cid` via SHA-256) — no medium it cannot rasterize. `voice` owns one deterministic thing, `voice_text_to_phonemes`; the neural TTS/STT sit behind `voice_set_tts`/`voice_set_stt` and **fail closed** (`VOICE_ERR_NO_ENGINE`, not one fabricated sample). `games` is a phase-tick state machine where `game_state_hash` gives lockstep P2P, and `game_companion_decide` acts *through* `chg_infer`, holding position when unbound.

Composition is the whole design: `wyverneye`'s `wyvern_read` stacks the Refinery on Wyrmgate so intent always forges but the *action* is judged (S+ commit / S0 defer / S- reject), yielding anti-manipulation for free; `mage`'s hat/engagement gate (`mage_authorize`, expiry in phase ticks) authorizes any capability the suite exposes.

## 10. Networking, P2P & Content Addressing

This domain moves bytes between sovereign nodes without ever letting the wire dictate what those bytes *are*. It spans a radio/transport substrate, a self-organizing mesh, a content-addressed object layer, a fraud-proof marketplace, and two coordinators that stitch heterogeneous cells and ISAs into one constellation.

**Transport substrate — JDR PirateNet.** `jdr_piratenet` models a virtual SDR as hardware-as-code: `jdr_network_t` owns up to 32 `jdr_transceiver_t` register-file devices spanning every band (`jdr_band_t`, ULF→neutrino→harmonic) with DMA rings, IRQ lines, and FHSS hop tables. `jdr_transceiver_create`, `jdr_transmit`, `jdr_hum_compute`, and `jdr_fhss_next_freq` are the public surface. The harmonic-hum carrier registers (`reg_hum_freq`, `harmonics[16]`, `coverage_ratio`) are `surplus_real_t` — Q32.32 on target via the `SR_` macros, so carrier math never touches float on the freestanding target.

**Mesh & routing.** `mesh_net` is explicitly *not* TCP/IP: peers are 168-bit `word168_t` identities, and a `mesh_net_t` holds user-created `mn_network_t` meshes plus priced `mn_route_t` trade routes. `mn_create_network`/`mn_join_network` gate admission through a `porter_house_t *` (join can return `-3` on Porter rejection), `mn_net_access_t` ranges OPEN→PRIVATE, and `price_per_unit` settles in Vino vouchers — the One Policy at work: routes are voluntary, priced, revocable, never extractive. `p2p_caracho` (the Carracho-style direct-stream node — handshake `"TCPZEDEC"`, DHT tracker, LPRES 5-state stream recovery) is **superseded**; its header marks the live implementation as `bootlegger`, kept only because tooling cannot delete it.

**Content addressing — the load-bearing invariant.** `ipfs` makes a CID *exactly* one SHA-256 digest (`IPFS_CID_LEN == 32`), reusing the kernel's real `sha256`, never a private hash. The defining guarantee is *self-certification*: `ipfs_get_verify` recomputes SHA-256 over exactly the bytes that came back and returns `IPFS_ERR_CID_MISMATCH` if they don't hash to the requested CID — no port authority can rename or seize what is addressed by its own fingerprint. Transport is an OPS BOUNDARY (`ipfs_transport_t`, a reused `upd_transport_t`); with no adapter bound every fetch is `IPFS_ERR_NO_TRANSPORT` — bytes are never fabricated. Integer-only, no allocation.

**Marketplace — Mister Shanghai's broker.** `broker` lists any information product by its 32-byte CID under a seller-signed `aipic_contract_t`. Delivery (`broker_deliver`) routes through `ipfs_get_verify`, inheriting fraud-proofing verbatim. Trust is the buyer's, not the House's: an author must be on `broker_trust_author`'s allow-list AND pass a bound `broker_verify_fn` (bind `ed25519_verify`) — unbound fails closed (`BROKER_ERR_NO_VERIFY`). Access is gated by a hardened capability handshake (`aipi_hs_begin`→`_challenge`→`_prove`→`_allow`) whose grant hinges on a constant-time HMAC over the whole transcript (`aipi_hs_allow`), with freshness driven by a verifiable monotonic beacon phase, never wall-clock. Settlement is its own OPS BOUNDARY (`broker_settlement_t`) — no confirming backend, no fill. `tribute_split` is honestly labeled app-layer economics, not a security primitive.

**Coordinators.** `constellation_coordinator` (`cc_coordinator_t`) is capability-based node discovery, schema routing (`cc_route_event`, `cc_find_executor`), and timeout failure detection (`cc_check_health`); nodes enter `CC_NODE_QUARANTINED` until healthy, and no coordinator is a permanent single point of failure. `cellular_multikernel` (`cell_fabric_t`) admits fault-contained `cell_t`s carrying `crit168_value_t` digests and coordinates via the Tri-Space triad — `fabric_event_t.lane` is S+/S-/S0, resolved by `triad_commit_update`. Its `cell_fabric_discover` carries the **red-team OOB fix (CRITICAL, 2026-08-04)**: candidate `cell_id` must be NUL-terminated and `transport_count`/`supported_schema_count` clamped to their array bounds *before* any loop dereferences them — an unclamped count was a control-plane memory-corruption primitive.

**Composition.** `ipfs_install_as_bridge_resolver` wires the CID layer into `bridge`'s `resolve_cid` slot, so one classifier (`bridge_classify`) spans Web2 DNS / Web3 CID / Web4 Chiglet intent, each an unbound-fails-closed backend. `event_transport` is the physical bridge under the event-space fabric that both coordinators speak; `ai_layer` reuses 168-bit content hashes, signed packages, and Porter House admission — the same primitives, composed rather than reinvented.

## 11. Languages, Legacy Compatibility & the Terminal

This domain brings dead languages, future languages, and the human operator to one phase-tick floor. Its through-line: never fabricate a translation. Every bridge is either exact and asserted, or it fails closed at an OPS BOUNDARY.

**The compatibility spine — one IR, not N×N bridges.** `orbital_compat` defines a canonical typed record `oc_ir_t` whose fields (`oc_field_t`) carry a normalized type plus an *exact* value: the numeric normal form is `rat_t` (WyvernEye exact rational), so `123.45` lowered from a COBOL COMP-3 field and from a Fortran fixed-format field is the same rational bit-for-bit. Each language is described once — `oc_lower()` in, `oc_lift()` out — via a bound `oc_lang_ops_t`. An unbound language is simply absent and returns `OC_ERR_NO_LANG`; `oc_register_builtins()` deliberately leaves `OC_LANG_SUTRA`/`OC_LANG_FUTURE` unbound to keep the fail-closed behaviour demonstrable. The IR is a phase-tick dataflow record: a field's identity is its value, ordered by registration, never a wall clock.

**Lightning Rod — the measured lowerings underneath.** `orbital_compat`'s COBOL/Fortran/C adapters reuse `lightningrod`, a *representation adapter* (not a transpiler). It converts across the conventions that actually break integration — packed/zoned decimal, EBCDIC, column-major vs row-major, 1-based vs 0-based. Every conversion returns `lr_result_t` whose load-bearing field is `exact`: `lr_packed_to_rat()` turns COMP-3 straight into `rat_t` (exact by construction, so a migrated ledger balances with the mainframe), while `lr_double_bits_to_rat()` reports `exact=false` — the conversion naive migrations silently corrupt. `lr_bridge_exists()` reports "no safe bridge" rather than guessing.

**Sutra — the AI/nonlinear-COBOL front-end.** `sutra` is a language whose runtime interprets its AST directly against a real `vino_ledger_t`; it reuses `capital_type_t`, `payment_rail_t`, `rational_t` rather than reinventing them. Steps are `@ORDINAL(N)`-tagged and dispatched in ordinal order, not line order (`sutra_run()`); a fused `DEBIT`+`CREDIT` pair compiles to a single atomic `vino_transfer()`. `sutra_parse()`/`sutra_self_audit()` refuse silent partial parses, and out-of-scope features are declared, not faked.

**Orbital Elevator — event-schema, not instruction, translation.** `orbital_elevator` translates *event envelopes* across schema versions/ABIs through a signed adapter graph. `oe_translate()` finds a bounded path (`OE_MAX_HOPS = 8`); with no path it returns `OE_TRANSLATE_NO_PATH` rather than corrupting, and untranslatable fields are marked unknown (paraconsistent) rather than dropped.

**zBIOS — the ten-key measured boot.** `zbios` is a chain-of-trust: ten keys in three triads (SILICON/STORAGE/SOVEREIGNTY) plus `ZB_K9_SEAL`. `zb_stage()` enforces order, caller-supplied signature validity, and a rollback floor, extending a hash chain `M[k+1]=SHA256(M[k]||digest||index)`; any tamper, reorder, or skip changes `zb_seal()`'s attestation quote. A failed stage HALTS — no continue-anyway path.

**The terminal.** `pterm` is a freestanding console multiplexer (framebuffer + UART, no termios) with a phase-aware trit prompt. `pterm_mux` weights sub-terminals not by timeslice but by the Interaction Surplus Framework: `g(u)=1+(N-1)u` in Q16.16, recomputed each `pmux_phase_tick()`, so orthogonal work earns more dispatch. `subterm` adds a subterminal tree where `subterm_spawn_synced()` groups share one integer clock — `subterm_tick()` advances all members in lockstep. `xedit` is a gap-buffer editor tracking a live `cid`/`merkle_root`.

**Tri-Space file types.** Every ZXV program ships as a triad, not one file: positive `S+` (`.zxvc`), negative `S-` (`.cedez`), neutral `S0` (`.cedec`). `trispace`/`dual_space` enforce five hard requirements in `tri_bind()` — all three bound, S- never over-capable versus S+, S0 holds no production effect until a signed policy resolves it, irreversible effects must declare a compensating/constraining/observational inverse, generated S- labelled as draft — else the triad is QUARANTINED into S0. `fusion` treats S+ as a capability *plug* (provides) and S- as a *socket* (needs); `fuse_compose()` connects them associatively, refusing `FUSE_ERR_UNMET` when nothing plugs the socket. `pungent`'s garlic/onion tiers map onto these same file roles, so privacy posture is a first-class artifact property, not a runtime afterthought.

## 12. Platform, Desktop, Display & Pirate Apps

This domain is the surface ZXV presents to a person: the document scaffold apps are built on, the living desktop, the multi-language text stack, the themed iconography, the software rasteriser beneath it all, hardware modeled *as code*, and the "pirate" application constellation. Its governing discipline is that a window is `"a porthole onto a capability that is real or honestly dark"` — the UI holds no capability of its own and never paints a success a backing module did not return.

**AppKit — the document model.** Every native app (Writer, Sheet, Deck, Sound, Reel) sits on one `doc_t`: a fixed `DOC_CAPACITY` gap buffer plus bounded undo/redo rings. `doc_insert`/`doc_delete` are O(1)-amortised at the caret; consecutive typing *coalesces* into one undo record so `doc_undo` behaves as a person expects. Apps differ only in how they *interpret* the buffer, never in how editing or persistence work. Freestanding throughout — an edit that would exceed capacity fails rather than truncating.

**Shimmer — the living ground.** `shimmer_t` is the house's key architectural exemplar: its *entire* animation state is a single `phase` counter advanced by `shimmer_advance()` once per event cycle — never a wall clock. The field is a sum of three incommensurate travelling plane waves plus a domain warp, sampled per pixel with a 256-entry `shm_sin` LUT in integer Q8.8. Identical event sequence ⇒ identical frames (`shimmer_shade` composites over a base ARGB), keeping it inside the phase-tick and external-clock-bridge rules.

**Fonts / i18n.** The hard part isn't drawing glyphs, it's deciding *which script and direction* each stretch of mixed text is. `font_itemize` (in `script.h`) decodes UTF-8 and cuts a string into single-script `font_run_t`s; `font_resolve` maps a run's `font_script_t` to a registered `font_face_t` from the 109-face ASCW set. The glyph boundary is stated honestly: `font_glyph` renders through a bound TrueType backend (`font_bind_ttf`) or a builtin 8×8 diagnostic subset, and returns `false` — never a fabricated box — for a codepoint it cannot draw yet.

**Icons / theme — the house style.** `theme_t` makes the palette override-able tokens (`THEME_GOLD`, `THEME_ACCENT`, `THEME_VOID`…) so nothing hard-codes a colour; `theme_set` on any slot recolours everything at once. **Hardware-as-code.** `rtl_device_t` describes kernel modules as register/DMA/IRQ devices that can emit Chisel/SystemVerilog/VHDL via `rtl_device_generate_hdl`, with `rtl_gate_create`/`rtl_gate_annihilate` as second-quantization operators. `dlp_projector_t` models the Tank line's DLP Pico projector with real, checkable optics — time-of-flight autofocus (`dlp_projector_laser_measure`), N-way wobulation, photometric lux — advanced only by `dlp_projector_tick()` off the phase-coordinator stream, the same clocked-hardware/event-space boundary as the GICv3 timer PPI. ## 13. Cross-Cutting Invariants

A handful of invariants are not owned by any one layer; they are the connective tissue that lets the layers compose without leaking.

- **The `surplus_real_t` contract.** Every layer speaks the same Q32.32 number. A value minted in the economy layer, weighted by a reputation score, priced by a market, and allocated by the sustainable allocator never changes representation and never rounds inconsistently — because none of them ever leaves the `SR_` macros. Host builds prove the arithmetic against `double`; a dedicated non-`TEST_HOST` test (`test_alloc_q32`) proves the *target* Q32.32 path against known answers, catching overflow the host build masks.
- **Ops-boundary fail-closed, everywhere value or trust moves.** Escrow release (logistics), outcome distribution (battering_ram), delivery attestation, credential activation (crown), and rail settlement (vino) all take a bindable verifier and refuse when it is unbound. This is one pattern applied uniformly; a reviewer can find every trust boundary by finding every `*_set_verifier` / bindable hook.
- **Conservation identities as tests.** Splits sum exactly to their total; a redistribution's shares sum to the pool; a swap conserves principal across two distinct forms; equity equals `debit − credit` or the post is usury. These identities are asserted, not assumed.
- **Composition over duplication.** The One Policy is written once and *composed* by zcapital, vino, ministry, logistics, battering_ram, and crown. Concord's matching math is written once and *composed* by social_spaces and pirate_fleet. Chiglet's mixture-of-experts is written once and *composed* by the companion, the refinery, and the games. No layer re-implements another's core.

## 14. Security Posture

The system's trust model is **verify the signature, never the fact; fail closed when unsure**. Post-quantum readiness is first-class: key establishment uses **ML-KEM** (`mlkem`), and credential/attestation checks use **Ed25519** with a canonical-`S` malleability guard. Content is self-certifying — CIDs are SHA-256 over the bytes (`ipfs`), so an artifact is its own name.

Two adversarial red-team gates have been run against the tree, each as a multi-agent workflow of independent finders followed by skeptics who tried to *refute* every finding, with a frozen regression test per confirmed fix:

- **Kernel-layer gate** (commit `953e3ae`): 20 raised, **19 confirmed and fixed**, 1 refuted. Included a CRITICAL out-of-bounds in the cellular-multikernel discovery path, signed-shift UB in the fixed-point core that *runs on the real target*, an unauthenticated filesystem-journal write-what-where, and Ed25519 `S`-malleability. The untrusted-input surfaces built to be "safe by construction" (`zca`, `refinery`) raised **zero** findings.
- **Economy-layer gate** (commit `15f5455`): **12 confirmed and fixed**. The CRITICAL was a logistics escrow that released funds and minted reputation on a bare caller-set `confirmed` boolean; it now requires a bound Ed25519 delivery verifier, contract-party membership, and a goods witness, and fails closed otherwise. Three HIGH findings closed a voucher double-redeem, a *unilateral* quarantine (one reporter could quarantine anyone — now real distinct-reporter consensus is required), and a `soc_sort` shadowban (synthesised reports — removed). A subtle MEDIUM hardened the One Policy so its unilateral-extraction check fires on the modeled values *regardless of the attacker-controlled `reciprocal` flag*; another closed a money-round-trip mint on the exchange (`BR_ERR_NO_RAIL`).

The guards from both gates are load-bearing and are never to be removed; their regressions live in the per-module `test_*.c` and run under `-fsanitize=address,undefined` in the host build.
