# Prior art index

The ZXV hardware designs are published as a defensive publication: openly,
with dated archives, so that they are prior art and cannot be patented by
anyone. Each entry records where and when a disclosure was published.

| # | Disclosure | First published (UTC) | Archives (DOI / URL) | SHA-256 |
|---|------------|-----------------------|----------------------|---------|

Archives used for each release: the tagged GitHub release, Zenodo (DOI),
Software Heritage, and Technical Disclosure Commons.
