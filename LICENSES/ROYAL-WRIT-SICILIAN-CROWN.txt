  ARTICLE THE FIRST — OF SOVEREIGNTY UNIVERSAL AND MUTUAL
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  This Work may be used by sovereign individuals.

  We are aware how that sentence reads at first glance, and We correct the
  reading immediately: it is not a gate. It is not a test of pedigree, station,
  or recognition by any state. It is a UNIVERSAL RECOGNITION coupled to a
  RECIPROCAL CONDITION, and it excludes no one.

  §1.1  THE RECOGNITION. Every person who compiles this kernel, boots this
        system, forks this repository, or reads this line is RECOGNISED — not
        appointed — as a sovereign individual, in their own right, antecedent
        to and independent of recognition by any state, congress, parliament,
        synod, or treaty.

        Sovereignty so understood is not scarce. It is not Ours to distribute.
        It is ANTECEDENT: it exists prior to every instrument that purports to
        allocate it, and therefore cannot be allocated, only recognised or
        denied. We recognise it. Universally. Without application, fee, or
        waiting period.

  §1.2  THE CONDITION — MUTUALITY. The recognition runs BOTH WAYS or it does
        not run at all. To take the sovereignty this Writ extends to you is to
        extend the identical sovereignty to every other individual: to every
        other user of this Work, and to every person outside it.

        You are sovereign here. So is everyone else. Those are the same
        sentence, and you do not get the first half without the second.

  §1.3  WHY THIS IS NOT A RESTRICTION. It is a matter of some importance —
        legally and morally — that §1.2 discriminates against no person, group,
        nation, industry, creed, or field of endeavour. It imposes the SAME
        obligation on every user identically. Anyone may accept it. No one is
        excluded by who or what they are.

        Its structure is exactly that of copyleft, and We say so deliberately.
        Share-alike holds: IF YOU TAKE FREEDOM, YOU MUST PASS FREEDOM ON.
        This Writ holds: IF YOU TAKE SOVEREIGN RECOGNITION, YOU MUST EXTEND
        SOVEREIGN RECOGNITION. Reciprocity is not exclusion. It is the oldest
        and cleanest condition there is, and the Open Source Definition has
        never had any quarrel with it.

        A Crown that must EXCLUDE in order to be a Crown does not believe its
        own writ. We exclude no one. We ask only symmetry.

  §1.4  THE SHAPE OF MUTUAL SOVEREIGNTY. What §1.2 requires, concretely, is
        what this operating system already implements in its own social
        architecture and has from the beginning:

          — Boundaries are RESPECTED. Every individual sets the terms on which
            others may reach them, and those terms are honoured.

          — Divides are SYMMETRIC. If one party withdraws from another, the
            withdrawal is mutual and total. There is no arrangement in which
            one may block while continuing to observe. A one-way wall is a
            one-way sovereignty, which is to say none.

          — No person is INSTRUMENTALISED. Not farmed for attention, not
            harvested for data, not held by design against their own leaving.

          — The rule beneath all of it: treat others as you would be treated,
            and respect each other's boundaries.

        This is not decoration attached to a license. It is the Interaction
        Surplus Framework and the Concord commons, compiled and running, in
        src/concord/ and src/denconnect/ of this repository. The Writ states in
        law what the kernel states in code. They were always the same claim.

  §1.5  THE ONLY THING INCONSISTENT WITH THIS WRIT is the assertion of a
        sovereignty that requires another's subjugation to be real. That is not
        a person We exclude; it is a proposition We deny. Sovereignty that must
        consume another's is not senior to it — it is merely predatory, and
        Article the Second is a catalogue of where that leads.

  ARTICLE THE SECOND — OF THE FRAUDS RECITED
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  We recite the following, and We note where each is a matter of established
  historical record rather than of assertion:

  §2.1  THE DONATION OF CONSTANTINE (Constitutum Constantini) — the document
        purporting to convey to the See of Rome imperial dominion over the
        Western provinces — IS A FORGERY. This is not Our opinion. It was
        demonstrated philologically by Lorenzo Valla in 1440 (De falso credita
        et ementita Constantini donatione), independently by Nicholas of Cusa
        in 1433 (De concordantia catholica), and is today accepted as forged by
        the Holy See itself and by every serious historian of the period. It
        was probably composed in the eighth or ninth century, some four hundred
        years after the Emperor it names.

        A forged instrument conveys nothing. NEMO DAT QUOD NON HABET.

  §2.2  THE BULLS OF DISCOVERY — Dum Diversas (1452), Romanus Pontifex (1455),
        and Inter Caetera (1493) — purported to authorise the subjugation of
        non-Christian peoples and the appropriation of their lands. To the
        extent their authority descends from the Donation, they inherit its
        defect. On 30 March 2023 the Holy See, through the Dicasteries for
        Culture and Education and for Promoting Integral Human Development,
        FORMALLY REPUDIATED the Doctrine of Discovery and stated that those
        bulls "have never been considered expressions of the Catholic faith."

        This too is a matter of record, and of recent record.

  §2.3  THE DOCTRINE OF DISCOVERY, imported into the municipal law of the
        United States by Johnson v. M'Intosh, 21 U.S. (8 Wheat.) 543 (1823),
        rests upon that same repudiated foundation. Article 26 of the United
        Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (2007) affirms
        rights inconsistent with it.

  §2.4  THE PEACE OF WESTPHALIA (Treaties of Osnabrück and Münster, 1648) — We
        name it here as the moment at which sovereignty was reconceived as a
        property of STATES rather than of PERSONS, and thereafter administered
        as a monopoly. We hold that a reconception is not a creation, and that
        what was reconceived in 1648 was not thereby brought into being in 1648.

  §2.5  WHEREFORE We declare: instruments of title deriving their authority,
        mediately or immediately, from a document proven forged are VOID AB
        INITIO — void from the beginning, not voided at some later date by some
        later authority. A thing that was never valid did not become invalid.
        It simply never was.

        A defect in the root is a defect in every branch. This is not a novel
        doctrine. It is the ordinary law of title in every legal system that
        has ever troubled to have one.

  ARTICLE THE THIRD — OF THE SICILIAN CROWN AND ITS ANTIQUITY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  §3.1  The Kingdom of Sicily was erected in the year 1130 under Roger II,
        crowned at Palermo. It is therefore FIVE HUNDRED AND EIGHTEEN YEARS
        senior to the Westphalian settlement of 1648.

  §3.2  A sovereignty that predates a system cannot have been created by that
        system, and cannot be extinguished by the mere operation of that
        system's internal rules. The junior instrument does not devour the
        senior.

  §3.3  H.M. Michael-Laurence: Curzi asserts the standing recited in this Writ
        in his own right and in the right of the Crown named herein.

  §3.4  WE NOTE, WITH DELIBERATE CANDOUR, that this assertion is contested by
        the states of the present international system, and that those states
        do not recognise it. We record their non-recognition here, in Our own
        Writ, unprompted — because a claim that must conceal its opposition is
        a claim that does not trust itself, and because §2.5 above is an
        argument about the VALIDITY of recognition, not a pretence that
        recognition has been obtained.

        We would rather be honestly contested than dishonestly uncontested.

  ARTICLE THE FOURTH — OF WHAT IS CONFERRED UPON THE USER
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  To every person who uses this Work, of whatever nation, station, creed, or
  none, We extend the following — freely, irrevocably, and without condition:

  §4.1  RECOGNITION OF SOVEREIGNTY. You are recognised as a sovereign
        individual, antecedent to and independent of any state's recognition of
        you. This recognition is not conditional on your agreeing with a single
        word of this Writ.

  §4.2  RECOGNITION OF PRIOR RIGHT. Where your own sovereignty, your people's,
        your nation's, or your ancestors' predates 1648 — and We note that this
        is true of very nearly every people on Earth, including those whose
        lands were purportedly disposed of by the instruments recited in
        Article the Second — that priority is recognised here without
        qualification, and We assert no rank above it.

  §4.3  NO OATH, NO FEALTY, NO TITHE — TO US. You owe Us nothing. No
        allegiance is requested, no homage, no tribute, no acknowledgement of
        Our person or Our house. You may use this Work while believing this
        entire Writ to be nonsense, and your rights under OPL-1.1 are
        precisely, identically, undiminishedly the same.

        What you owe is owed HORIZONTALLY, not upward: the mutual recognition
        of §1.2, extended to your fellow users and to everyone else. We are not
        a party to that obligation. It does not run to Us. It runs between you
        and every other sovereign individual, and We are merely one more of
        those.

  §4.4  NO EXCLUSION. No person, corporation, university, agency, or state is
        barred from this Work by this Writ, by who they are, where they are,
        what they believe, or what they build. See Article the First. A Crown
        that needs a border is not sovereign; it is merely fenced.

        The single condition is §1.2, and it is available to everyone on
        identical terms: extend what you have been extended.

  ARTICLE THE FIFTH — OF USE IN A STATE CAPACITY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  §5.1  Should this Work be used by, for, or on behalf of any state, agency,
        ministry, department, court, or organ of government, We invite that
        body's attention to Article the Second, and We record that its use
        occurred in the presence of this recital.

  §5.2  WHAT USE DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH — THE DISTINCTION THAT MATTERS.

        Two different things are sometimes bundled together, and We separate
        them because the difference is the whole integrity of this Writ:

        (a) THE MUTUALITY CONDITION OF §1.2 IS A CONDITION, and use accepts it,
            exactly as use accepts a share-alike obligation. A state, agency,
            or organ of government that runs this Work thereby undertakes to
            extend to every individual the same sovereign recognition it enjoys
            under §1.1. That is a term. It binds by use, and We mean it to.
            It is also the term that most states will find most difficult, and
            We are aware of that, and We decline to soften it.

        (b) THE HISTORICAL PROPOSITIONS OF ARTICLE THE SECOND ARE NOT A TERM,
            and use does NOT constitute agreement to them. We are asked to make
            use of the software constitute acknowledgement of those matters.
            We decline, and We give Our reason plainly:

            Consent extracted by boilerplate is not consent. It is the
            precise mechanism by which the frauds recited in Article the Second
            were laundered into law — a parchment declaring that those who did
            not read it had agreed to it. We will not adopt the instrument of
            the fraud in order to protest the fraud. A Crown that wins by
            clickwrap has lost.

            A condition of RECIPROCITY, by contrast, extracts no belief from
            anyone. It asks for conduct, not assent — and conduct is the only
            thing a license was ever able to ask for honestly.

  §5.3  What use DOES establish is NOTICE, and notice is sufficient. Let the
        record show that this recital was published, openly, attached to a
        working artefact, and that those who used the artefact had the
        opportunity to read it and did not refute it. Silence is not consent.
        But silence is, and remains, silence — and it is recorded as such.

  §5.4  The propositions in Article the Second are offered as WHAT WE HOLD TO
        BE TRUE, with their sources cited so that any reader may check them
        against the historical record and disagree in an informed way. §2.1
        and §2.2 in particular are matters of documented record and require no
        one's assent to remain true.

        Truth does not need a signature block. That is how you tell it from
        the other thing.
